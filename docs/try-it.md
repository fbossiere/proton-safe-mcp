# Try Proton Safe on a useful task

[Français](fr/essayer.md)

Choose one conversation you are comfortable sharing with your AI provider. Allow about
15 minutes for a first attempt; this is a suggestion, not an installation-time guarantee.
[Check the requirements and install the app](download.md) first.

## 1. Check the connection

In your local assistant, ask:

> Check whether Proton Safe tools are available, without reading my messages or creating a draft.

If the tools are missing, return to Proton Safe and check the connection. Follow the
client-restart instructions before reinstalling anything.

## 2. Find and summarise a conversation

Replace the bracketed topic with your own:

> Find my recent conversations about [topic] and summarise the questions still open.

This request reads your mail. Check the summary against the original exchange. Was it accurate,
and did it save you time? You do not need to share the exchange or the summary in your feedback.

## 3. Prepare a reply you can review

> Suggest a short reply to that exchange. Show me the recipient, subject and exact body before creating a draft.

Review the proposal and confirm the exact content only if you want the draft created.
Open Proton Mail to check the draft. **You decide whether to send it.** Sending is not
required for this test, and Proton Safe cannot send it for you.

## Send feedback

[Share your experience on GitHub](https://github.com/fbossiere/proton-safe-mcp/issues/new?template=desktop-feedback.yml){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

The form is **public and requires a GitHub account**. French and English are welcome.
Report your Ubuntu version, Proton Safe version, assistant, the last successful step and
what was useful or confusing. No email content is needed.

Do not include account addresses, passwords, message text, attachments or unredacted
screenshots/logs. The app offers a redacted diagnostic preview; review even that before
sharing. For a suspected security issue, use the
[private reporting route](https://github.com/fbossiere/proton-safe-mcp/security/advisories/new).

If the maintainer invited you personally and you prefer not to use GitHub, reply through
that same private conversation with the step you reached and what blocked you. There is no
mailing list or separate registration form.

After a few days, try a second real task. Tell us whether you used the connection again;
that is more useful than a download count or a star.
